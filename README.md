# Trees of Joy 2026 – shopping guide

A one-page shopping guide for decorating a Harry Potter themed tree (pizza topper; red, green & blue) for the Make-A-Wish Canada Trees of Joy event, plus gifts from the wish lists.

- Each section matches a line from our handwritten shopping list and has 5+ product options with images, prices, store names and links.
- Products come from independent Shopify stores (via the Shopify Catalog), Amazon.ca and Canadian Tire, since the sponsorship includes gift cards for both.
- The bottom bar tracks spending against the $1,000 target after the gift cards. Basket choices are saved in your browser only.

Prices collected Nov 6, 2026.
